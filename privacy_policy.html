Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

1. Introduction

Silent Library respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address and any message you send through our Contact Page form, or when you register interest in a library event or promotion. We do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, confirm event registrations, and send our monthly newsletter if you choose to subscribe. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

This website may use basic cookies needed for the site to function correctly, such as remembering your cookie preference. We do not use cookies for third party advertising or cross site tracking.

5. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, request that we correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us About This Policy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at hello@silentlibrary.sg or visit the Contact Page for our address and phone number.
