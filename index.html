About Silent Library

A quiet space for every reader

Silent Library is a community library dedicated to giving every reader a calm, welcoming place to read, borrow, and discover something new. Since opening on Prinsep Street, we have grown into a small but well loved corner of the neighbourhood, hosting story times, book clubs, and quiet study afternoons for readers of every age.
