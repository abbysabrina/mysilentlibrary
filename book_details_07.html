Where The Rivers Meet

Aisha Rahman

Available now

Description

Where The Rivers Meet is a collection of poems tracing rivers, borders and belonging, moving between the poet's childhood home and the cities she has since come to call home.

Written over ten years, the poems are short, plainspoken and unafraid of quiet, sitting comfortably with distance, homesickness and the small joys of arriving somewhere new.
