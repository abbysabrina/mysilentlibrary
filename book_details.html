The Quiet Hour

Mara Lindgren

Available now

Description

After the sudden loss of her husband, translator Mara Lindgren moves to a small coastal town to finish a manuscript she can no longer bear to read. Between long walks along the harbour and half finished cups of tea, she begins to translate not just words, but her own grief, one quiet hour at a time.

The Quiet Hour is a tender, unhurried novel about loss, language and the slow work of healing.
