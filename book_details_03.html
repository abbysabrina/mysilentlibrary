The Cartographer's Daughter

Priya Nathan

On loan

Description

Set in a coastal city during the last years of colonial rule, The Cartographer's Daughter follows Ines, who inherits her late father's mapmaking workshop and, with it, a set of old land surveys that do not match the stories she grew up with.

As she retraces her father's routes, Ines uncovers the quiet decisions that shaped the boundaries of her hometown, and the price paid for them. A patient, richly detailed story about memory, land and inheritance.
