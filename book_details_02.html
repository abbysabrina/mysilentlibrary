Small Kindnesses

Daniel Ocho

Available now

Description

Small Kindnesses follows Mei Lin, an office worker who begins leaving short, anonymous notes of encouragement around her city, tucked into library books, left on park benches and slipped under office doors.

As the notes travel from stranger to stranger, they quietly draw together a community that had drifted apart. A gentle, hopeful novel about the ripple effect of small, unseen acts of care.
